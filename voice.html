How to read this

Waveform — both sides share the same vertical scale, so taller bars = louder. Match your dynamics to the reference.
Spectrum — where the energy sits. If the reference peaks far to the right (bright), your take should too.
Pitch contour — the melody line. Compare shape, not just height: a good match has a similar curve.
Metrics row — green = you are above the reference, amber = below, grey = close enough.
